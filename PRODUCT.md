# Advay’s personal website
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A personal site introducing Advay Chandorkar, his experience, and projects. Visitors can read about him, explore individual projects, and follow links to contact him or see his work.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js Pages Router with React and TypeScript. Preserve existing photos, experience content, project assets, the drag-selection box, and access to the archived desktop site. Projects have an index and individual description pages with real images, with click-to-zoom previews. Sitemaker includes a compressed walkthrough GIF. Do not fabricate project results or screenshots. The timeline source is parked and unlinked while its content is being refined. Publishing requires the owner’s final approval.

## Brand Commitments
The owner requested a minimal, centered reading column inspired by the supplied screenshots, keeping Helvetica Neue and the original #171717 background. Only gray, black, white, and cream backgrounds are offered. Keep a restrained orange accent. Remove the decorative mountain footer, background-pattern controls, and font switching from the redesigned interface.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing project content in src/lib/fileConfigs.ts and src/lib/projects.ts, experience content in src/lib/experience.ts, portraits in public/icons, project previews in public/projects, and interactive diagrams at /systems. Wrapped uses a labeled rendering of the project with synthetic sample data.
